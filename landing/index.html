01 / The proposition

One structure.
Several kinds
of meaning.

A probability distribution, a memory operation and a quantum channel can share a way of connecting. They cannot inherit the same laws by accident.

U gives every operation an interface, a theory, a resource account and an observation boundary. Composition becomes a checked mathematical relationship. The laws travel with the operation as it moves from source to graph, from graph to execution, and from execution to evidence.

The intended reach spans ordinary programs, scientific models, concurrent systems and physical-process descriptions. The current repository is the first reference implementation of that architecture, with each supported fragment and remaining obligation visible.

Read the architecture ↗

Three objects. Three different questions.

I

Meaning

What does this operation permit?

A typed graph, its theory, observations, assumptions and numeric contract. An equation retains its equation semantics before any solver runs.

II

Realization

How is it carried out here?

A particular evaluator, compiler, solver or device. Changing the implementation preserves meaning only when its full semantic contract stays fixed.

III

Evidence

What has actually been earned?

A checked proof, executed result, verified comparison or explicit hold. Each claim is bound to its subject, method and remaining obligations.

02 / Structural kernel

Six ways to compose.
No concealed seventh world.

The structure is small. Every generator still owes its full semantic account.

A ⊗ B → B ⊗ A

Connect what already exists.

Forward or permute existing typed ports. Wiring preserves the interface's resource account; copying, discarding, casting and observing require their own admitted operations.

A wire does not duplicate a quantum register or an owned allocation.

03 / Mathematical worlds

Keep the distinctions
that do the work.

Seven families organize obligations. They are not a claim of a globally minimal foundation.

Ownership, borrowing, allocation, access and order.

Identity is more than contents.

Unification, constraints, answer order, bag multiplicity.

A relation is more than one search.
P

Weighting, conditioning, normalization and inference.

A sample is not its distribution.

Behavior & clocks

Causality, messages, schedules, signals and failures.

One trace is not every behavior.

Equations, trajectories, resets, derivatives and frames.

A numerical step is not an exact flow.

Joint quantum state, gates, measurement and reset.

A simulator is not a physical device.

05 / The bridge has a contract

Meaning survives
only when the
right things survive.

Choose a bit, then toss a fair coin: the best chance of matching is ½. See the coin first, then choose: it is 1.

Probability and nondeterminism cannot simply exchange places. The observation policy is part of the program. U requires an admitted composite theory or a checked adapter with an interface map, preservation relation, assumptions and loss account.

The readable source can remain concise. Deterministic elaboration makes the boundary evidence explicit in the graph, whether it was written by hand or inferred.

FAITHFUL IMPORTsource → graph + residual + provenance

Exact source reconstruction and semantic preservation are separate obligations. Retaining every byte does not itself establish understanding of the imported program.

06 / Built from an existing line of work

CDC came first.
U asks what lies beneath.

BiDi's Coherence-Delta Calculus connects source, native state, guarded effects, variation and receipts. U generalizes that discipline into a wider language.

This is a new implementation relationship. CDC was never secretly implemented in U. Its pinned native semantics remain an independent reference, and its balanced-ternary barrier, cover geometry and phase laws remain specialized contracts.

CDC source→U1 acceptance→U2 tangent→Recurrence gate

A tangent can be valid while recurrence is held. That hold preserves what was earned and prevents unsupported monodromy or multipliers from appearing. U carries the same discipline into its general evidence architecture.

07 / Formal manuscript

A claim should
show its premises.

The accompanying paper gives explicit definitions and scoped arguments for structural preservation, conservative independence, adapter composition, artifact identity, residual reconstruction and CDC's recurrence-gated analysis.

Its mathematical statements are conditional on their declared models. They are not presented as a mechanized proof of the entire implementation. The manuscript is prepared for author review and intended for arXiv; it has not been submitted.

08 / Where the work stands

Full ambition.
Visible obligations.

The repository contains the stage-0 reference implementation, preserved design lineage, tests, source examples, tooling and this manuscript. Support is assessed per operation and per realization.

The current evidence ledger governs the release boundary. Full foreign-language subsumption, production completeness, self-hosting and physical backends require additional gates. A supported reference or compiled fragment does not earn those broader claims.
