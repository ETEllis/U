\begin{abstract}
U is a research language and semantic substrate organized around operations
whose composition laws, resource interfaces, observations and evidential
obligations remain explicit. A six-constructor structural calculus connects
admitted generators without identifying their mathematical models. Seven
theory families account for constructive values and proofs, resources and
memory, relations and search, measures and probability, behaviors and clocks,
continuous dynamics, and quantum channels. The proposal separates an
operation's meaning from its realization and from the evidence produced by
that realization. It requires checked composite profiles or adapters at
mixed-theory boundaries, deterministic elaboration, conservative independence
witnesses, distinct artifact identities, and residual-preserving source
ingestion. We give conditional structural-preservation results, a finite
resource nonduplication argument, a relational adapter-composition theorem
with explicit assumptions, a quantitative error-composition bound, identity
and invalidation lemmas, and an earned-artifact non-fabrication theorem. The
Coherence-Delta Calculus supplies a demanding case study: its existing finite
maps, scheduled resets and nested updates must be preserved before their
derivatives can authorize recurrence-gated return analysis. CDC predates U;
the proposed specialization is a new implementation relationship. An explicit
Python stage-0 reference implementation accompanies the design. The
mathematical results concern stated abstract models and do not constitute a
mechanized correctness proof of the whole implementation. Neither complete
foreign-language subsumption, globally minimal foundations, self-hosting,
production completeness nor physical-backend execution follows from the
present artifact.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and contribution}

A common representation is useful only if it preserves the distinctions that
make the represented systems meaningful. An integer value, a mutable
allocation, a search relation, a probability measure and a quantum channel can
all appear behind a function-shaped interface. That fact does not authorize
the same rules of copying, observation, reordering or execution for all of
them. Similarly, a numerical trajectory and a differential equation may be
connected without denoting the same mathematical object.

U takes the operation at an interface as its structural unit. An operation
includes a semantic profile, the resources and effects visible at its
boundary, the observations under which its behavior is assessed, and the
rules by which it may compose with other operations. A realization implements
that contract in a specific evaluator, compiler, solver or device. Evidence
records what was established about that realization and its outputs. These
objects are connected by explicit dependencies rather than merged into a
single status field.

The proposal is motivated by an existing implementation lineage. In
\bidi{}, \cdc{} connects canonical source to native state transitions,
guarded effects and receipts; U1 adds lifted-cover acceptance and U2 adds
variation followed by recurrence-qualified return analysis
\cite{ellis-bidi}. U abstracts a broader composition discipline after that
work. The historical order matters: \cdc{} was not previously implemented
in U, and the existence of a native \cdc{} runtime cannot serve as evidence
that a new U realization has preserved its semantics.

The contribution is an integration protocol and a concrete proof program,
not the invention of typed graphs, algebraic effects, linear resources or
categorical composition. The paper develops four connected results:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a structural calculus whose admitted profiles retain domain-specific
  law premises and whose derived operations remain inspectable;
\item compositional obligations for resource independence and cross-theory
  adapters, including explicit assumption and approximation accounting;
\item identities and evidence rules that distinguish a represented meaning,
  its implementation, its execution and its earned consequences; and
\item a compatibility case study that retains \cdc{}'s actual finite maps,
  derivatives, discrete modes and recurrence refusal.
\end{enumerate}

The ambition is a useful general programming language and semantic substrate.
The design excludes an exact terminating evaluator for every admitted
mathematical description, an automatic equivalence decision for arbitrary
source languages, and the inference of physical execution from simulation.
Those exclusions follow from the meaning of the proposed contracts; they
are not temporary performance limitations.

\subsection{Three discriminating examples}

\paragraph{Copying.} A reusable integer admits a diagonal operation
$x\mapsto(x,x)$. An owned resource does not acquire a second owner because
its handle appears twice. Unknown quantum data does not acquire a general
copy operation because its circuit description is classical data. Wiring
must therefore lack implicit contraction.

\paragraph{Observation order.} Let $C$ be a fair coin. A decision $b$ made
without observing $C$ satisfies $\Pr[b=C]=1/2$, including randomized choices
independent of $C$. If the decision observes $C$, the policy $b=C$ achieves
probability one. These programs use the same ingredients with different
information access. A transformation that interchanges probability and
choice without preserving the observation policy is unsound.

\paragraph{Finite time steps.} In a scalar instance of the pinned \cdc{}
flow map, $F_d(x)=x+d\cos x$. Consequently,
\[
 F_1(F_1(0))=1+\cos 1 \ne 2=F_2(0).
\]
The exact-flow semigroup law is not available for this realization. A U
import that replaces it with an ideal ODE has changed the operation that
\cdc{} actually executes.

\section{Semantic objects and admission}
\label{sec:objects}

\begin{definition}[Semantic profile]
A profile $T$ specifies a class of interfaces $\mathcal I_T$, a class of
operation meanings $\mathcal M_T(A,B)$ for admissible boundaries, an
observation family $\mathcal O_T$, a signature of generators, their laws and
side conditions, numeric and temporal conventions, and its assumptions.
It also specifies which structural constructors it interprets and which
compositions are defined.
\end{definition}

This definition does not require every model to be a set-theoretic total
function. A profile may use partial maps, relations, measures, behavior
sets, path spaces or quantum instruments. It must state its observation
relation explicitly. Two profiles can interpret an apparent common carrier
using different observables; a shared type name alone does not identify them.

\begin{definition}[Operation descriptor]
A generator descriptor is a tuple
\[
 o=(A,B,T,\epsilon,u,s,\mathcal L,r,\mathcal O,\mathcal A,\mathcal D),
\]
where $A,B$ are interfaces, $\epsilon$ is the effect interface, $u$ the
resource-usage account, $s$ a stage, $\mathcal L$ the applicable laws and
their premises, $r$ a direct rule or checked expansion, $\mathcal O$ the
observations, $\mathcal A$ the assumptions and $\mathcal D$ immutable
dependencies. The descriptor also records failure and hold conditions and
the trusted implementation/checker boundary.
\end{definition}

The distinction between direct and derived operations is accountable, not
cosmetic. A derived SQL join retains a visible join identity and a checked
expansion dependency; lowering it to smaller operations need not erase that
identity. Conversely, naming a foreign callback \texttt{join} does not
establish a U relational semantics.

\subsection{Admission is a product of dimensions}

For an operation $o$, an admission record has the shape
\[
\operatorname{Adm}(o)=
(\operatorname{origin},\operatorname{support},
 \operatorname{laws},\operatorname{assumptions},\operatorname{trust}).
\]
Origin can identify a primitive, derived definition, library operation or
foreign import. Support is indexed by realization: reference evaluation,
compiled code, simulator or actual device. Laws identify particular checked
statements, not a blanket proof badge. Trust lists checker and implementation
dependencies. An admitted primitive can have a checked law and an unresolved
assumption while lacking a hardware backend. Collapsing these fields into one
exclusive enumeration loses information needed to judge a composition.

The initial seven families, V/M/R/P/C/D/Q, organize this accounting. They do
not assert seven irreducible axioms. The cost of generators, type rules,
models, search strategies, solver assumptions and trusted code must remain
visible beneath each family. Structural brevity is a separate measurement
from semantic, implementation and human comprehension costs.

\subsection{Configuration and outcomes}

A reference configuration may be written
\[
 K=(G,\eta,S,\Delta,E,\kappa,\Xi),
\]
with graph $G$, bindings $\eta$, stores $S$, resource ledger $\Delta$,
event structure $E$, continuation $\kappa$ and evidence context $\Xi$.
A store is an indexed carrier appropriate to its profile, not a promise
that physical quantum state or every continuous trajectory is a classical
serializable heap value.

An outcome distinguishes successful completion, semantic refusal, invalid
input, execution failure and uncertainty about an external effect:
\begin{align*}
\operatorname{Outcome}(A)={}&\done(A,\text{receipt})\\
 &+\hold(\text{obligations},\text{earned artifacts})\\
 &+\mathsf{Rejected}(\text{diagnostic})\\
 &+\mathsf{Fault}(\text{error},\text{known progress})\\
 &+\mathsf{Indeterminate}(\text{recovery obligation},\text{known progress}).
\end{align*}
A hold protects the state designated by the refused operation. It need not
roll back earlier effects, remove an audit event or restore consumed attempt
identities. A budget interruption does not decide mathematical divergence.

\section{Structural calculus and deterministic elaboration}
\label{sec:structure}

For admitted theory environment $\Theta$, the structural syntax is
\begin{align*}
p ::= {}&\wire[\pi]\mid\gen[T,o](\vec a;\vec r)
 \mid\seq(p,p)\mid\parop(p,p)\\
 &\mid\scope(n,p)\mid\fix[m](x,p).
\end{align*}
Here $\pi$ permutes compatible ports; $\vec a$ are static arguments;
$\vec r$ are typed, staged regions; $n$ is a nominal binder; and $m$ names
an admitted iteration discipline. This presentation is economical, not a
proof that no five-tag presentation could encode the same information.

Use the judgment
\[
 \Theta;\Gamma;\Delta\vdash p:A
 \xrightarrow[T,\epsilon,u,s]{}B.
\]
$\Gamma$ contains reusable bindings and $\Delta$ affine or linear resources.
Sequential composition connects the same boundary under the same admitted
profile; a mixed boundary is first mediated by an explicit adapter. Parallel
composition requires an independent-resource judgment. Scope binds names
without capture or inadmissible escape. Iteration is qualified: partial
computation, monotone relational iteration, clock-guarded feedback and total
structural recursion have different premises.

\subsection{Local obligations imply a structural theorem}

Fix one admitted profile and its environment. Assume:
\begin{enumerate}
\item each generator reduction preserves its declared interface and usage;
\item wiring is a type-preserving permutation with no copying or dropping;
\item serial reduction composes only matching interfaces;
\item parallel reduction respects its valid split and does not modify the
  other branch's exclusive resources;
\item substitution is capture-avoiding and preserves typing and usage; and
\item the selected iteration discipline admits its unfolding or feedback
  rule with the same input/output contract.
\end{enumerate}
These are admission obligations on a profile, not conclusions of graph
shape alone. In particular, a generator with an incorrect local rule defeats
the theorem's assumptions.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional structural interface preservation]
Under the six assumptions above, if $p$ has boundary $A\to B$ and
resource account $\Delta$, a well-defined structural reduction of $p$
preserves that boundary and the profile's resource invariant, up to the
explicitly admitted consumption or creation of resources in generator rules.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the derivation of the reduction. A generator step uses assumption
1; a wiring reduction uses assumption 2. A left serial-context step changes
only its subderivation, so its output remains the unchanged right operation's
input by induction and assumption 3. The right-context case is symmetric
after the left result has been obtained. A parallel-context step preserves
its local boundary by induction and leaves the other resource partition
untouched by assumption 4, so the tensor boundary and split are retained.
Scope reduction uses freshness and assumption 5 to prevent capture or
escape. Iteration unfolding is a permitted substitution with the same
boundary by assumptions 5 and 6. These cases exhaust the structural rules;
none permits an unadmitted generator or a boundary cast.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This is a compositional metatheorem. It is neither a complete subject-reduction
proof for every proposed U domain nor a proof of the Python implementation.
Progress additionally requires each active generator to make a valid step,
return a permitted outcome, or be allowed to wait. Event waits, partial
recursion and unsupported solvers preclude a universal termination theorem.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Whole-surface elaboration}

Let $s$ be a parsed source tree, $M$ a fully resolved immutable manifest,
and $v$ an elaboration version. Define
\[
 \mathcal E_v(s,M)=(G,B,W,U),
\]
where $G$ is the graph, $B$ explicit bindings, $W$ inferred adapter and
profile evidence, and $U$ unresolved obligations. Source definitions,
records, calls, lets, regions, staged syntax and derived operations all need
versioned rules. A parser accepting their spelling is insufficient.

\begin{proposition}[Deterministic elaboration contract]
If the parser, name resolution, overload selection, traversal order,
binder naming, adapter selection, expansion lookup and serialization are
deterministic functions of $(s,M,v)$, repeated successful elaboration yields
identical canonical bytes.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Induct over the finite syntax tree. The leaf rules choose identical resolved
identities. At an internal node, induction gives identical elaborated
children; deterministic rule selection, binder construction and witness
generation then give identical parent objects. The root objects are equal,
and deterministic canonical serialization preserves equality.
\end{proof}

The premises explicitly exclude environment-dependent lookup, mutable package
aliases, unordered iteration with observable serialization, and selection
among ambiguous adapters without a rule. A fresh-name counter must derive
from deterministic traversal rather than an ambient process clock. The
result is deterministic representation, not uniqueness up to all semantic
equivalences. An implementation must test this proposition's concrete
premises rather than cite the proposition as evidence they were implemented.

\section{Resources and conservative independence}
\label{sec:resources}

Let $\mathcal R$ be a finite set of nominal resource identities. A footprint
for operation $p$ consists of owned resources $O_p$, reads $R_p$, writes
$W_p$ and an effect/observation contract. Read and write sets denote actual
resource identities after alias resolution. A read may use an owned resource
or a separately admitted shared capability. Unknown aliasing produces an
obligation rather than an assumption of disjointness.

\begin{definition}[First independence witness]
For explicit shared-read set $S$, define $I_S(p,q)$ by
\begin{align*}
 O_p\cap O_q&=\varnothing,\\
 W_p\cap(R_q\cup W_q)&=\varnothing,\\
 W_q\cap(R_p\cup W_p)&=\varnothing,\\
 R_p\cap R_q&\subseteq S.
\end{align*}
The witness is valid only if its identities, footprints and read permissions
are checked and the composite theory profile is admitted.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Owned-resource nonduplication in the finite model]
Suppose the initial ledger assigns each linear resource at most one owner,
every allocation creates a fresh identity, ownership transfer consumes the
old owning permission, and every parallel split satisfies $I_S$. No finite
execution composed of those steps contains two live owning permissions for
one linear resource.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the number of allocation, transfer, consumption and split steps.
The initial property holds by assumption. Allocation adds an owner of an
identity absent from the ledger. Transfer removes the old permission before
inserting its replacement. Consumption removes a permission. A parallel
split partitions owned identities into disjoint sets, so an identity cannot
be assigned to both branches; shared-read witnesses add no owner. Joining
the partitions preserves disjointness because each branch is constrained by
the same rules and fresh allocation. These cases preserve the invariant.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The model trusts identity freshness and footprint correctness. It does not
prove a hardware memory model, arbitrary alias analysis, distributed lease
freshness or physical no-cloning. An implementation whose user can forge
owning handles violates the premises. A read capability and an authority
lease remain different contracts even when both have a scope.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Qualified commuting updates]
Let $p,q$ be deterministic terminating store transformations with correct
finite footprints. Suppose their reads include every store dependency,
their writes contain every changed location, $I_S(p,q)$ holds, allocation
and external effects are absent, and observations compare final stores and
branch results without exposing order. Then executing $p;q$ and $q;p$
produces the same observed result.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Neither transformation changes any value read by the other, so determinism
gives the same branch result and proposed writes in either order. Their
write sets are disjoint, hence applying those updates in either order yields
the same final store. The stipulated observation ignores execution order.
\end{proof}

This lemma explains why disjoint writes can support one particular
optimization. It does not license the same rewrite when an observer sees
event order, operations allocate nominal resources, time matters, or a
device has unmodeled shared state. Named causal edges generally establish
dependence, not the independence certificate needed by the lemma.

\section{Adapters, assumptions and approximation}
\label{sec:adapters}

An adapter connects profiles through a declared relation; it need not assert
an isomorphism. Typical boundaries include quantum measurement into a
classical record, a probability measure into a sampled trace, a differential
equation into a numerical trajectory, a store snapshot into a relational bag,
and a CDC phase into a balanced-ternary observation.

\begin{definition}[Relational adapter]
An adapter $\alpha:T\rightsquigarrow S$ consists of an interface map,
a translation on its admitted fragment, a relation
$\mathcal R_\alpha\subseteq\mathcal O_T\times\mathcal O_S$, assumptions
$A_\alpha$, a checker or explicitly trusted argument, a loss account and a
residual policy. Its preservation assertion says that every admitted
translated execution has the stated related source/target observations,
under $A_\alpha$ and the chosen quantifier direction.
\end{definition}

Quantifier direction matters. Equality of all behavior sets, simulation,
trace refinement, one paired execution and statistical comparison are
different statements. A deterministic scheduler may refine permitted
nondeterminism while failing to preserve all source schedules. An adapter
must not report that refinement as equivalence.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional relational composition]
Suppose $\alpha:T\rightsquigarrow S$ and
$\beta:S\rightsquigarrow V$ agree on the intermediate interface,
observation semantics and translated object. Suppose their preservation
assertions apply to that same intermediate execution, with compatible
quantifier directions. Under $A_\alpha\cup A_\beta$, the composite has
preservation relation
\[
 \mathcal R_{\beta\alpha}
 =\{(x,z)\mid \exists y.\ (x,y)\in\mathcal R_\alpha
                    \land(y,z)\in\mathcal R_\beta\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any admitted composite execution, the second preservation assertion
provides its intermediate observation $y$ related to final observation $z$.
Compatibility makes this the intermediate object governed by the first
assertion, which provides $x$ with $(x,y)\in\mathcal R_\alpha$. Therefore
$y$ witnesses membership of $(x,z)$ in the displayed relation. Both
preservation uses retain their premises, so the combined assertion depends
on their union. No assumption is discharged by composition alone.
\end{proof}

The theorem is deliberately conditional on a compatible intermediate
observation and quantifiers. Two isolated success receipts cannot prove
that compatibility. If one adapter hides information on which the second
depends, a new contract is required. Likewise, reducing the written
assumption set needs a checked implication or proof discharge; deleting a
field from metadata does not strengthen a theorem.

\begin{proposition}[A quantitative special case]
Let $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$ be ideal maps between metric spaces, with
realizations $\hat f,\hat g$. On an admitted domain suppose
\[
 d_Y(\hat f(x),f(x))\le\epsilon_f,\qquad
 d_Z(\hat g(y),g(y))\le\epsilon_g,
\]
and $g$ is $L_g$-Lipschitz on a domain containing $f(x)$ and $\hat f(x)$.
Then
\[
 d_Z(\hat g(\hat f(x)),g(f(x)))
 \le \epsilon_g+L_g\epsilon_f.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Insert $g(\hat f(x))$ and use the triangle inequality. The first distance
is at most $\epsilon_g$ by the uniform realization guarantee at
$\hat f(x)$; the second is at most $L_g\epsilon_f$ by Lipschitz continuity.
\end{proof}

A tolerance slider supplies neither a uniform error bound nor the required
domain containment and Lipschitz constant. This proposition therefore gives
an admissible composition rule when those facts are available, and an
explicit obligation when they are not. Stochastic, hybrid and relational
adapters may need different metrics and preservation relations.

\section{A total proof boundary}
\label{sec:proof}

U's design permits programs to search, diverge, use IO and request solvers.
Its logical conversion relation must have a smaller boundary. Proof terms
may share some syntax with programs without inheriting unrestricted
evaluation rights. A tactic can return a candidate whose acceptance is
independently checked; a successful host callback cannot construct the
authority-bearing result merely by returning its name.

Consider a small illustrative arithmetic fragment with natural numbers,
successor, addition recursing on the second argument, equality,
reflexivity, congruence and induction. Its defining equations are
\[
 \operatorname{add}(a,0)=a,\qquad
 \operatorname{add}(a,Sb)=S(\operatorname{add}(a,b)).
\]
For concrete natural inputs, the recursive evaluation decreases the second
argument on each addition call. Equality proofs use these equations and
the declared inference rules; general partial fixed points are absent.

\begin{proposition}[An inductive witness]
For every $n\in\N$, $\operatorname{add}(0,n)=n$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
At zero, the left side reduces to zero. Assume
$\operatorname{add}(0,k)=k$. By the successor equation and congruence,
$\operatorname{add}(0,Sk)=S(\operatorname{add}(0,k))=Sk$. Natural-number
induction concludes the statement.
\end{proof}

This example is intentionally not reflexivity at an arbitrary symbolic
$n$: addition reduces on its second argument, so the universal statement
needs induction. A checker that treats the spelling of a proposed proof
as sufficient has skipped the central operation.

\begin{proposition}[Constructor-bounded proof authority]
Suppose the only way to create an accepted proof artifact is a checker that
recursively validates a finite derivation using a fixed set of sound rules,
with conversion performed solely by its terminating admitted conversion
procedure. Suppose its proof-artifact constructor is inaccessible to
untrusted programs. Then every artifact it issues has a valid derivation
under its recorded assumptions; a partial computation's result alone is
insufficient to obtain one.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the finite checked derivation. Each leaf is a valid axiom or a
recorded assumption. For an internal node, induction establishes its
premises and the sound checked rule establishes the conclusion. The
terminating conversion procedure only permits its declared equalities.
Constructor exclusivity excludes creation outside this validation path.
\end{proof}

The theorem does not independently establish soundness or termination of an
arbitrary extensible dependent type theory. Those are premises to discharge
for each actual checker. Python object privacy is also not an adversarial
process-isolation theorem. The stage-0 trust boundary includes its
implementation and execution environment. Full universes, positivity,
dependent elimination and metatheoretic correspondence remain separate
release obligations beyond any smaller checked fragment.

\section{Identity, dependencies and invalidation}
\label{sec:identity}

An artifact needs more than one identity. Original-source identity binds
exact bytes and the source environment. Canonical-syntax identity binds
normalized syntax and its version. Meaning identity binds graph, profiles,
numeric and observation conventions, expansion dependencies and assumptions.
Realization identity binds implementation. Execution identity also binds
input and relevant scheduler, randomness or external decisions. Replay
identity may select a declared projection of history. Resource identity is
nominal and can include an epoch; equal contents do not imply equal resources.

\begin{definition}[Canonical dependency identity]
Let $D$ be a finite directed acyclic graph of canonical objects, whose edges
point to immutable dependencies. For object $v$, define
\[
 h(v)=\hash\bigl(\canon(\operatorname{tag}(v),
       \operatorname{payload}(v),[h(w)]_{w\in\dep(v)})\bigr),
\]
with an explicit dependency ordering or canonical set ordering. Recursive
syntax is represented by binders and references within finite objects,
not by circular content-hash equations.
\end{definition}

The canonical encoding must be unambiguous for its domain. Numeric values
need a declared encoding, including finite IEEE bit patterns where relevant,
and an explicit policy for signed zero, nonfinite values and mathematical
integers. A serializer that conflates these values cannot preserve a profile
that distinguishes them. Human-readable receipt rounding can coexist with
an exact-bit sidecar; it cannot silently redefine legacy receipt identity.

\begin{lemma}[Representation identity and backend separation]
If two builds have identical canonical meaning objects and dependencies,
they have equal meaning digests. A realization change outside those objects
can preserve the meaning digest while changing realization identity.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Evaluate the DAG in a topological order. Equal leaf encodings have equal
digests; identical parent encodings and child digests then give equal parent
digests. The realization's encoding is a distinct object, so changing it
does not alter the fixed meaning object.
\end{proof}

The premise includes the whole semantic contract. Changing precision,
rounding, observation order or time behavior that the profile exposes is a
meaning-object change, even if described informally as a backend swap.
Meaning identity is identity of a representation and contract; it is not a
decision procedure for arbitrary extensional equivalence.

\begin{lemma}[Dependency invalidation under a finite collision assumption]
Assume the encoding is injective on encountered objects and the digest has
no collisions among their encodings. If an object's payload or a referenced
dependency changes, its digest changes, and the change propagates to every
ancestor whose canonical object retains that dependency. A receipt bound
to the old root cannot validate the new root by digest equality.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At the changed object, injective encoding gives different bytes and the
finite collision assumption gives different digests. At each retained
parent edge, the changed child digest changes the canonical parent bytes;
the same reasoning changes the parent digest. Induct on distance to each
ancestor. The new root digest differs, so equality with the receipt's old
subject fails.
\end{proof}

This lemma assumes dependency completeness. Omitting a law expansion, numeric
profile or external contract from the dependency graph can leave an invalid
receipt apparently current. Cryptographic collision resistance does not
repair that modeling error. A digest also provides neither sender
authentication nor truthfulness. Signed statements can be false, and a
commitment to discarded history cannot reconstruct that history.

\section{Earned artifacts and guarded consequences}
\label{sec:evidence}

For a fixed semantic subject $s$, let $\mathcal A_s$ be a set of possible
artifact kinds with a prerequisite relation. For example, an accepted path
may precede a tangent, a tangent plus recurrence may precede monodromy, and
monodromy plus a valid spectrum computation may precede multipliers. The
prerequisite relation is acyclic within a finite admission decision.

\begin{definition}[Evidence transition]
A state is $(s,E,O)$, with earned artifact set $E\subseteq\mathcal A_s$
and outstanding obligations $O$. An earning step may add $a$ only when a
trusted constructor validates its subject identity, every prerequisite is
in $E$, and its own gate succeeds. A held step retains $E$ and records the
unsatisfied obligation. Invalidation for a changed subject creates a new
subject context; it does not relabel old evidence as evidence of the new one.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Monotonic earning and non-fabrication]
For finite transitions governed by the definition, earned sets are monotone
within one fixed subject. Every earned artifact has an earned prerequisite
chain and a successful own gate. If an indispensable prerequisite is never
earned, no artifact requiring it can be earned.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on transitions. A held step retains the set. An earning step adds a
single artifact and removes none, proving monotonicity. Its constructor
checks all prerequisites and its own gate; induction supplies the valid
chains behind those prerequisites. If a required predecessor is absent,
the insertion precondition is false, preventing that artifact and, by
the same argument along the dependency chain, its dependents.
\end{proof}

The set model captures a guarantee about what is authorized, not a guarantee
that every gate implementation is correct or every observation truthful.
Those remain trusted dependencies. Artifacts can also expire or become
inadmissible for a new request; that is a new validity decision, not a
retroactive claim that a historical computation never happened.

The \cdc{} example shows why an ordered Boolean success flag is inadequate.
If recurrence fails, a valid tangent remains earned but monodromy and
multipliers are absent. If recurrence and monodromy pass and a spectral
backend is unavailable, monodromy remains earned while multipliers are
held. A downstream refusal neither promotes weaker evidence nor destroys
an independently valid earlier artifact.

\subsection{Effects and durability}

An admitted external effect adds obligations beyond the abstract artifact
rule: capability scope, freshness, causal parent, replay protection and
failure atomicity appropriate to that effect. Memory atomicity, a local
durable transaction and a distributed command are not one universal
primitive. A timeout after sending a command can leave completion
indeterminate. Exactly-once external action does not follow from deterministic
local replay.

This distinction follows the pinned \bidi{} toolchain and RFTC contracts.
Local journal replay and authenticated envelopes have particular mechanisms
and counterexamples; their presence does not establish a deployed multi-host
protocol or a physical law. U preserves these as distinct implementation
gates rather than inheriting their strongest possible interpretation.

\section{Residuals and native semantic possession}
\label{sec:residuals}

An import must declare a source fragment and an observational relation.
For source bytes $P$, define
\[
 \operatorname{lift}(P)=(G,R,\Pi),
\]
where $G$ is a typed U graph, $R$ a residual and $\Pi$ provenance. Residuals
may retain lexical layout, identifiers and intensional structure, unmodeled
semantic regions, environment assumptions and approximation data. Retaining
a source region does not imply U can execute or analyze it natively.

\begin{definition}[Exact reconstruction]
A lift/reconstruction pair is exact on fragment $F$ if for every $P\in F$,
\[
 \operatorname{reconstruct}(\operatorname{lift}(P))=P
\]
as bytes. Semantic preservation is a separate judgment relating the source
and target observation sets under their named profiles.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Reconstruction is insufficient for possession]
Exact reconstruction alone entails no semantic interpretation of the source.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Define a lift that maps every $P$ to one constant graph and stores the whole
$P$ in its residual. Reconstruction returns that residual. The equation
holds for every source, although the graph contains no distinctions among
their behaviors. Thus the equation cannot imply semantic interpretation.
\end{proof}

For structured edits, let $g:S\to V$ extract a view and
$p:V\times S\to S$ put an updated view into an old source. On an admitted
domain, the familiar lens laws are
\[
 p(g(s),s)=s,\qquad g(p(v,s))=v.
\]
They connect preservation of unchanged source with faithful propagation of a
changed view; bidirectional transformation work supplies the relevant
background \cite{foster-lenses}.

\begin{proposition}[Scoped reconstruction after edits]
If $g,p$ satisfy the two displayed laws on their admitted source/view domain,
an unchanged view reconstructs the same source, and putting a valid changed
view yields a source whose extracted view is that change. If semantic
interpretation factors through $g$ on that domain, the reconstructed source
has the changed view's semantics.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The first two conclusions are the two laws instantiated at $s,v$. For the
last conclusion, write source interpretation as $J\circ g$; then
$J(g(p(v,s)))=J(v)$ by the second law.
\end{proof}

Factoring through the view is a substantial assumption. If behavior depends
on residual content omitted from the view, the final conclusion is
unavailable. A transformation without a valid residual-update rule must
invalidate exact export or return a narrower guarantee. Reattaching stale
comments or old tokens to a changed graph cannot certify reconstruction.

\subsection{A fragment-indexed possession criterion}

For an admitted source fragment, native possession requires six kinds of
evidence: explicit target operators; compositional meanings; direct U rules
or checked expansions; analysis of those operators; recoverability with
declared loss; and differential/adversarial preservation checks. A hidden
source-language interpreter behind one generic node fails this criterion
even when it computes the correct output. Turing completeness says nothing
about the representation preserving source laws in an inspectable form.

Different source fragments need different relations. C and assembly need
memory/ABI and defined-behavior boundaries; Haskell needs laziness and bottom;
SQL needs bags, NULL and collation; temporal models need behavior and fairness;
probabilistic programs need distributions and inference assumptions;
hardware needs cycle and event-region semantics; quantum programs need
joint-channel and measurement semantics. No one test transcript proves all
of these relations.

\section{CDC as a later specialization}
\label{sec:cdc}

The three primitive native reductions are flow, commit and nest. Frameworks,

Unconsumed declarations remain obligations rather than extra silently

\subsection{Synchronous flow and its actual Jacobian}

For selected cell $i$, native flow uses one pre-step snapshot:
\begin{equation}
 \theta_i' = \theta_i + \omega_i d
 +Gd\sum_{e:t(e)=i}w_e
 \sin(\theta_{s(e)}+\alpha_e-\theta_i).
 \label{eq:cdcflow}
\end{equation}
Only channels admitted by the pinned field/profile contract contribute.
Parameters are frozen for this derivative. Direct differentiation gives
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\partial\theta_i'}{\partial\theta_\ell}
 =\delta_{i\ell}+Gd\sum_{e:t(e)=i}w_e
 \cos(\theta_{s(e)}+\alpha_e-\theta_i)
 (\delta_{s(e)\ell}-\delta_{i\ell}).
 \label{eq:cdcjac}
\end{equation}
The analytic derivative describes the real extension of the finite-step
expression in Eq.~\eqref{eq:cdcflow}, not the exact solution map of a
continuous equation or a derivative of literal IEEE rounding. In particular, a
self-channel's phase differences have the cancellation expressed by the
two Kronecker terms. Reversing or sequentializing the pre-step reads
changes the map and its Jacobian.

The numeric realization has an additional exact-bit contract. In the
inspected arm64 Apple Clang 21 \texttt{-O2} oracle, generated fused
multiply-add instructions make some expressions observably different from
separate multiplication and addition. The U compatibility path therefore
uses explicit fusion at the corresponding sites and requires Python 3.13's
\texttt{math.fma}. Compiler flags and contraction policy belong in the
realization/profile record. Matching binary64 storage alone is insufficient
to establish bitwise parity. Analytic finite-map differentiation and
numerical agreement with that pinned implementation are separate claims;
the former does not differentiate the discontinuities of rounded machine
arithmetic.

\subsection{Commit and nest in a fixed discrete itinerary}

With deadband $\delta$, native quantization is
\[
 \tau(\theta)=\begin{cases}
 +1&\cos\theta>\delta,\\
 -1&\cos\theta<-\delta,\\
 0&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
An ordered trit sequence is accepted only when every prefix sum is
nonnegative. A scheduled accepted commit writes latch state while leaving
the continuous coordinates unchanged. Its continuous reset derivative is
identity within a fixed accepted itinerary. At a quantization boundary or
when perturbation changes the discrete itinerary, that local derivative
does not establish a derivative across the branch boundary.

Let $u(q)$ be the child mean trit under the pinned latch/fallback rule.
Within fixed discrete mode $q$, nest executes
\[
 b_p'=b_p+G u(q),\qquad p_c'=b_p'.
\]
The new child prior depends on the old parent belief, not on its own old
prior. With respect to $(b_p,p_c)$ the affected Jacobian block is
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The zeros assume fixed trits and frozen gain. A derivative of a richer
theoretical feedback model is not a substitute for the derivative of this
assignment.

\subsection{Scheduled events versus localized guards}

If an event is scheduled by the source, the reset derivative is $DR$ under
that schedule. A state-localized transverse guard may instead require
saltation. Under differentiability, fixed itinerary, guard
$g(t,x)=0$ with $n=\nabla_xg$, reset $R(t,x)$ and nonzero denominator,
the saltation expression has the form
\[
 S=DR+
 \frac{(f^+-DRf^- -\partial_tR)n^{\mathsf T}}
 {n^{\mathsf T}f^-+\partial_tg}.
\]
The localized event contract must establish the actual guard, crossing,
vector fields and reset. A scheduled commit does not become such a
localized event because a saltation formula is available. Grazing, missing
event localization or altered order blocks the corresponding derivative
claim. The pinned U2 contract makes these distinctions explicit
\cite{ellis-bidi}.

\subsection{Tangent, recurrence and return}

Let the accepted finite path be
$F=F_n\circ\cdots\circ F_1$ on a source-bound coordinate manifest.
For column tangents, initialize $A_0=I$ and update
\[
 A_k=J_k A_{k-1},\qquad A_n=J_n\cdots J_1.
\]
The ordinary chain rule establishes this derivative wherever the admitted
local differentiability and fixed-itinerary conditions hold. Reversing the
product is generally wrong.

Absolute recurrence requires the full declared continuous endpoint to
return, together with discrete-mode restoration. Relative recurrence
requires an explicit restoration $\rho$, its derivative, its domain and
the requisite symmetry/equivariance contract. Only then is the relative
return derivative
\[
 M_{\mathrm{rel}}=D\rho(F(x_0))A_n.
\]
A projected phase match cannot certify complete return. A matrix can have
eigenvalues without those numbers being Floquet multipliers of a periodic
orbit. Numerical tolerance creates a profile-qualified recurrence test;
it is not an exact periodic-orbit theorem without further error analysis.

\begin{corollary}[Recurrence-gated non-fabrication]
If the evidence gate for monodromy requires a valid tangent and verified
full or admitted relative recurrence, then a recurrence hold prevents
monodromy and all dependent spectrum artifacts while retaining the valid
tangent. If monodromy is already earned and a spectral backend holds, the
monodromy remains earned.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply the prerequisite and monotonicity theorem of
Section~\ref{sec:evidence} to the tangent/recurrence/monodromy/spectrum graph.
\end{proof}

The pinned canonical two-turn loop illustrates the first case: lifted-cover
U1 closure and a tangent do not imply full-state return when unwrapped
phase, latch presence, belief and prior fail the return contract. The
separate relative-return calibration declares a $4\pi$ endpoint restoration
and its complete derivative. It is a different fixture, not a replacement
interpretation of the canonical loop. U must preserve both distinctions.

\subsection{Framework, toolchain and RFTC boundaries}

\bidi{}'s frameworks attach role contracts and executed exemplars to
existing machinery. U should retain their derived identities and checked
bindings, rather than counting every task-shaped name as a new universal
primitive. The toolchain's binding amendments distinguish canonical source
parsing, ABI ownership, content digests, keyed authority, crash durability,
per-check parity and trusted-local package execution.

The native self-hosting mandate is a historical removal program whose
current authority is qualified by the repository's release documents.
\bidi{}'s C runtime and minimal declaration oracle do not make U's Python

\subsection{Discriminating checks}

The verification program follows mechanisms and refusal conditions:
\begin{itemize}
\item elaborate repeated input with the same manifest to equal canonical
  bytes; change an expansion dependency and invalidate dependent evidence;
\item distinguish equal-named and equal-content resources from identical
  allocations; reject duplicate ownership, shared writers and escaped views;
\item preserve the coin/choice observation-order difference and SQL bag
  multiplicities; hold undefined normalization instead of inventing a posterior;
\item prevent partial recursion, unsafe coercion and external success
  strings from producing accepted proof objects;
\item compare direct evaluation with each admitted compiled fragment under
  the same overflow, ordering and numeric contract;
\item compare CDC state, decisions, effects and derivative products to the
  pinned independent implementation; reject false projected recurrence;
\item retain already earned artifacts when a later gate holds, while
  rejecting stale subject identity or fabricated prerequisite records; and
\item reconstruct original source bytes where promised, and refuse exact
  export after edits that lack a valid residual update.
\end{itemize}

These are acceptance criteria, not a claim that all have been discharged
merely by listing them. Unit, property, differential, mutation and security
checks have different scopes. A mutation family should be tied to an
actual mechanism: reversed Jacobian order, wrong nest derivative, omitted
restoration derivative, false recurrence, or a stale capability receipt.
Passing a finite sample is evidence about that sample and mechanism; it is
not an unbounded theorem.

\section{Related work and research position}

MLIR provides extensible typed operations, regions and progressive lowering
across compiler and application domains \cite{lattner-mlir}. U's shared
graph representation is therefore not by itself a novelty claim. The
research question concerns contracts made mandatory and inspectable across
meaning, realization, source reconstruction and evidence. A convincing
evaluation must compare the cost and capability of that protocol with
appropriate MLIR-based and domain-specific alternatives.

Algebraic effects and handlers already organize computation using operations,
equations and interpretations into models \cite{plotkin-handlers}. U builds
on the idea that interpretation must respect laws. Its proposed additional
boundary is an integrated user-visible account of resource authority,
physical realization, residuals and evidence. The paper does not claim
that all seven theory families are one algebraic theory or that every
combination admits a distributive law.

Structured cospans provide a principled account of open systems with
interfaces and compositional network models \cite{baez-courser}. They
support the plausibility of common structural composition across scientific
domains. U still owes concrete generators, operational rules, usability and
adapter checks; the availability of a categorical construction does not
automatically supply an executable language or the conditions for every
mixed-domain composition.

Bidirectional transformation and lens work studies disciplined extraction
and propagation of views \cite{foster-lenses}. U uses this perspective to
separate byte reconstruction from semantic lifting, and to make source
updates conditional on valid residual rules. The elementary lens proposition
here is an application of those laws, not a new result about lenses.

Verified compilation work, exemplified by CompCert, treats semantic
preservation as a theorem about concrete compiler passes and source/target
semantics \cite{leroy-compcert}. U's conditional metatheorems and tests do
not attain that level of whole-implementation correspondence. A future
proof must connect the implemented graph, checker, evaluator and compiler
passes to their formal models, with the bootstrap and external calls in
the trusted base.

OpenQASM 3 distinguishes quantum computation, classical control and timing
at multiple levels of circuit specificity \cite{cross-openqasm}. It provides
a concrete comparison target for the Q/C boundary. U must preserve a
declared OpenQASM fragment's semantics and execution assumptions before
claiming native support; a Bell-state simulation or emitted circuit file
does not establish that result for the full language or physical hardware.

The present originality claim is thus a research hypothesis about the
integrated protocol and its execution discipline. Demonstrating useful
compression without hiding domain semantics requires comparative artifacts,
preservation evidence, usability work and realistic maintenance accounting.

\section{Limitations and full-completion program}

The structural theorem depends on sound local generator rules and
substitution. The resource theorem depends on unforgeable ownership and
correct footprints. Adapter composition depends on a genuinely compatible
intermediate observation. Identity invalidation depends on complete
dependency capture and a finite collision assumption. Evidence
non-fabrication depends on correct constructors and gates. These premises
are obligations to implement and verify; writing them down does not prove
them of every execution environment.

The current work does not establish a globally minimal semantic basis,
automatic subsumption of arbitrary source languages, a complete dependent
type theory, a production distributed runtime, hostile-package isolation,
formal correspondence of all host code, physical GPU/HDL/QPU execution or
self-hosting. Classical simulation, interchange emission, numerical
calibration and physical realization are distinct support classes.

The original directive remains the completion target: foundational contracts
and canonical syntax; executable values, resources and proofs; native CDC
compatibility; theory conformance; faithful ingestion and law-aware
transforms; production classical runtime; specialized execution gates;
stronger formal correspondence; and staged self-hosting. Self-hosting requires
a U-written parser, formatter and elaborator, a stage-1 compiler, reproducible
stage-2 compilation, and continued checks against an independent oracle.
Deleting the oracle would weaken the result rather than complete it.

Progress should be measured independently in compile time, runtime,
memory, semantic coverage, representation density, proof/analysis coverage
and human comprehension. Performance comparisons require matched semantics,
numeric modes, hardware and workloads. A compact graph is not automatically
faster execution, and the ability to describe a physical process does not
establish a new physical law.
